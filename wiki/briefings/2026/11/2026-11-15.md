# AI Frontier — November 15, 2026

Reconstructed historical briefing; not an original contemporaneous transcript. Source cutoff: November 15, 2026.

## Engineering harnesses: EngineAgent

Candidate source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14356.

Hardware-aware validation is a promising approach to [[concepts/verified-completion-vs-apparent-success]]. A proposed experiment is to compare generic coding agents against a builder/auditor harness on embedded accelerator integration, using actual device execution and reference outputs as acceptance criteria.

## RTL optimization

Candidate source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13713.

A proposed experiment is to compare agent-generated RTL and synthesis changes against timing, area and functional equivalence constraints. Functional equivalence alone cannot establish equivalent side-channel or fault-injection resistance.

## Constrained taxonomy editing

Candidate source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13966.

A proposed experiment is to compare constrained taxonomy editing with unrestricted Markdown editing on hierarchy validity, duplicate entities, semantic coherence and execution cost.

## Coding-agent instruction compliance

Candidate source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13709.

Executable checks can complement natural-language repository instructions. A proposed experiment is to test generated validators against deliberately invalid and known-good fixtures, distinguishing validator correctness from agent compliance.
