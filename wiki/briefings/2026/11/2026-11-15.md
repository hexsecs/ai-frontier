# AI Frontier — November 15, 2026

Reconstructed historical briefing, not the original transcript. Source cutoff: November 15, 2026. Four November 10 preprints illustrate externally constrained engineering and knowledge work.

## 1. EngineAgent

Canonical research record: [[projects/engineagent|EngineAgent]].

[November 10 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14356v1).

A hierarchical index captures dependencies across inference layers. A builder and auditor use prevalidated backend procedures and device tests. Authors report nine of twelve tasks successful versus four for the strongest baseline, requiring compilation, device execution and reference-logit agreement.

**Evidence limit:** The sample covers three models and four engine/backend combinations; transfer to unrelated embedded systems is unproven. Author-reported results; not independently reproduced. Section 5.2 and Table 3 use a five-hour cap per task; the matched-model comparisons distinguish harness effects from model choice. Cost excludes non-API expenses.

**Practical experiment:** Compare generic and specialized agents on accelerator integration with reference-output checks.

## 2. Dr. RTL

Canonical research record: [[projects/dr-rtl|Dr. RTL]].

[November 10 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13713v1).

Agents analyze timing paths, rewrite RTL and evaluate implementation results while retaining reusable skills. Authors report mean WNS/TNS improvements of 21%/17% and area reduction of 6% across twenty designs relative to commercial synthesis.

**Evidence limit:** Timing and functional criteria do not establish side-channel or fault resistance. Author-reported results; not independently reproduced. Section 4.4, Table 2 reports a separate eight-design synthesis search: mean area and delay reductions of 10.6% and 9.1% against agent-tuned Yosys+ABC. These are not the twenty-design RTL results.

**Practical experiment:** Compare RTL changes under area, timing, equivalence and separate security tests.

## 3. TIGA

Canonical research record: [[projects/tiga|TIGA]].

[November 10 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13966v1).

An explicit taxonomy state, editing operations, interaction memory and adaptive policy constrain agent decisions. Four benchmark taxonomies and two model backbones show generally stronger structural results, mixed precision and higher cost.

**Evidence limit:** Structural validity does not establish semantic truth; global review benefit varies. Author-reported results; not independently reproduced. Section 6.3, Table 3 tests component removals over five seeds on two datasets. Removing memory hurts edge F1 on both; adding review helps WordNet but slightly hurts TExEval-2. Section 6.4 documents the cost trade-off.

**Practical experiment:** Compare constrained editing with unrestricted Markdown edits on duplicate rate, semantic quality and cost.

## 4. Coding-agent instruction adherence

Canonical research record: [[projects/coding-agent-instruction-adherence|Coding-agent instruction adherence]].

[November 10 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13709v1).

Researchers derive executable measurements from actionable context-file instructions and assess associated agent commits. Compliance varies substantially by instruction category and agent.

**Evidence limit:** Observable postconditions cover only a subset of possible instructions; a measurement script can itself be wrong. Author-reported results; not independently reproduced. Section 4.2, Table 6 reports 4,123 of 6,552 observable instructions operationalized; manual assessment found 67 of 94 accepted scripts faithful. Applicability leakage and overly strict scripts can bias compliance in opposite directions.

**Practical experiment:** Test generated validators against known-good and deliberately invalid fixtures.

## Synthesis

Device tests, implementation measurements, taxonomy constraints and instruction checks observe different properties. None alone guarantees task-wide correctness. [[concepts/verified-completion-vs-apparent-success]] distinguishes these acceptance boundaries. Compare these approaches on false acceptance as well as successful output and cost.
