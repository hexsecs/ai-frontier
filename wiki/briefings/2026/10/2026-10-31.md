## Retrospective addendum — 2026-11-02

*Added 2026-11-02 under issue #2, after every project this briefing covers was deepened to source level. The snapshot above is unaltered; this addendum records what verification did to its claims.*

One framing fact: the briefing made no claim about any covered project beyond listing it; its only interpretive claim is the frontier signal, "Compile learned procedures into tested programs and typed contracts." The verdicts below attach to the selection and the summary-level characterizations the import carried (metrics previously unverified).

### What held

- **SkillCompiler** — confirmed: well-specified operations become validated programs in a Program Asset Bank; the rest stays LLM reasoning. Per-model average margins run 6.02–11.90 points, author-reported.
- **WebAnything** — the automation claim held as reported for the historical 85-site subset: no recorded per-site manual implementation, debugging, or repair after configuration.
- **AgenticDS** — the contract-governed Planner–Engineer–Critic design is confirmed — and, in a rare cross-check, the repository's tables match the paper's Tables 2 and 3.
- **Seq@K** — the divergence claim held: across 26 model–benchmark pairs, resampling gains and feedback gains correlate at Spearman −0.08 once first attempts are removed.
- **Intent Loss in Smart-Contract Synthesis** — pre-generation elicitation adds 19–23 points (author-reported), and grading instruments miss planted errors: Slither caught 1.5% of 200 mutants.
- **FilingLoop** — confirmed at corpus scale: 380 tasks, 6,099 requirement occurrences, 76.5% disclosure changes that must appear in the filing.
- **HERA** — the blinded trial against a sealed expert conclusion ran as described, the curated corpus its central variable.
- **GARDEN** — confirmed: one LLM-engineered reward program, frozen and hashed, transferred unchanged to held-out scenarios over 10 matched seeds.

### What was corrected or qualified

- **SkillCompiler** — "up to 32.33% improvement" → one best model–benchmark cell (GLM-5.3-Flash on LiveMath); some cells are near-ties (DocVQA +0.50), and with no code or data linked, nothing is independently replicable.
- **WebAnything** — "244 reconstructed websites" → an inventory count, not a quality count: evidence covers roughly ten sites, 131 of 244 got no review beyond an HTTP 200 and a screenshot, and the widest gap (Historical Text F1 0.8541 vs. 0.2617) was computed with JavaScript disabled on the source side, enabled on the mirror side.
- **AgenticDS** — a neutral benchmark → HuDS-Bench's relational half is built from the same HuggingKG the system traverses (author-acknowledged), its utility result rests on three queries, and the "Claude Code"/"Codex" figures are the authors' GLM-5.2 harness configurations, not the vendors' products.
- **Seq@K** — feedback generally helps → the strongest results come from privileged feedback modes — rubric-aware critics, close to an oracle tutor; with rubric-blind critique, repair rates match no feedback, and a Seq@K value means nothing without its protocol specification.
- **Intent Loss in Smart-Contract Synthesis** — measuring "intent loss" → what is measured is implementation fidelity against the original contract; earlier summaries (and this page's earlier title) blurred the authors' distinction. The gains are optimistic by construction — the simulated client reads the very contract fidelity is scored against — and over all 279 planted mistakes the best oracle catches 65.2%, not 87.5%.
- **FilingLoop** — self-evolving improvement of +3.68 points → one model (gpt-6.1-sol) is backbone, grader, fact matcher, and skill editor; human–grader agreement is unquantified; the gain is one seeded stream, no interval, no held-out set.
- **HERA** — validated against expert judgment → the trial measures agreement with one expert (the senior author, also grader of record), and the second-expert check failed its sealed prediction: 65% exact agreement, κ = 0.38, against ≥0.70 predicted. Anthropic-funded while evaluating Claude models (declared; no role stated).
- **GARDEN** — reward engineering beats the controls by 5.7–10.7 points → inference compute was not matched: the search used 155 LLM calls and 15.0M input tokens against the controls' 18 calls and ~3.5M; one search per condition, and the flagship +68.8-point gain sits where native shaping reaches only 27.3%.

### What the briefing could not yet see

- Gains live in state around frozen models — asset bank, learned procedures, frozen reward program — [[concepts/improvement-lives-in-external-state]]; HERA against AgenticDS left capability's locus open — [[concepts/capability-locus]].
- Evaluators sit inside the systems they grade: FilingLoop's four-hat model, AgenticDS's shared graph, WebAnything's same-family builder and verifier — [[concepts/evaluator-circularity]].
- Scores are protocol-relative: a Seq@K value detached from its feedback mode does not travel — [[concepts/protocol-relative-scores]].
- Headline counts outran validation tier — 244 artifacts against ten validated, simulator-scored elicitation gains — [[concepts/validation-tier-gap]].
