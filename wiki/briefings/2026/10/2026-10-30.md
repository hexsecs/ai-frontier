## Retrospective addendum — 2026-11-02

*Added 2026-11-02 under issue #2, after every project this briefing covers was deepened to source level. The snapshot above is unaltered; this addendum records what verification did to its claims.*

One framing fact: the briefing made no claim about any covered project beyond listing it; its only interpretive claim is the frontier signal, "Trustworthy evidence before accumulating memory." The verdicts below attach to the selection and to the summary-level characterizations the import carried (whose metrics were previously unverified).

### What held

- **VeriCUA** — confirmed as a separate post-hoc verifier; on OSReward-Hard it is the only method improving on both metrics from flash to pro (accuracy 70.4 → 73.1, balanced accuracy 68.3 → 69.2), author-reported.
- **ExploreRepair** — the information-constrained setting is real: only 4.1% of 75,807 CVEs had a PoV within a year (cited figure); the system repairs from the repository plus a hint, building its own PoV.
- **ExLabel** — confirmed as an automated assessor of SWE-bench-style instances (task clarity, test-suite adequacy), experience bank included; its cleaning retained unanimous-agreement instances (κ = 0.928).
- **CodeSpecWeave** — confirmed; the headline figure stands as reported: 73.9% of pilot candidates had flawed tests.
- **A2UI and CopilotKit** — structure confirmed: four message types, declarative JSON composed from a client-declared catalog, rendered by the client's own native components; production version v0.9.1.
- **Auctopus** — the end-to-end investigation is confirmed: the calibration bound tightened from 91 to 10 measurements at L = 3 (L² + 1), and the system rejected its own first run on failed controls.
- **Repairs for Whom?** — the central prescription held: at equal rollout budget, stronger-model (14B) selection for the 7B executor loses 0.25 [0.19, 0.32] of net gain in ALFWorld — match the executor, not the strongest judge.
- **Security Impact of Coding Agent Memory Files** — the channel held: a planted project memory file demonstrably reaches the agent as trusted context — an indirect prompt-injection entry point.

### What was corrected or qualified

- **VeriCUA** — "independent" verification → independent of the executing agent only. Author-reported by the method's own team; most backbones are Google's, and two of five benchmarks (OSReward, ScienceBoard) are same-group — where the margins are most dramatic.
- **ExploreRepair** — headline margins → measured against adapted, weakened baselines: EvoRepair lost its ground-truth PoV and oracle feedback, its loop cut from 15 turns to 3; against LoopRepair with its PoV intact, the VulnLoc+ margin narrows from +20.0/+17.1 to +2.9/+11.4.
- **ExLabel** — a neutral quality benchmark → SWE-TQ was constructed and cleaned by the same team, around the very failure modes ExLabel targets, and its ground truth keeps only instances annotators already agreed on; performance on contested instances is unmeasured.
- **CodeSpecWeave** — verified benchmark figures → every number is author-reported, its tasks were manually reviewed by the authors themselves, and nothing is independently replicated.
- **A2UI and CopilotKit** — one product with demonstrated benefits → two artifacts with different owners (Google's protocol; CopilotKit's framework and transport), and no primary source contains a quantitative result: every benefit claim is an unquantified vendor claim.
- **Auctopus** — predictions committed before the experiment → the prediction lock came after data collection, before any target mean was computed (Methods 7.6): analysis-order discipline, not pre-registration. Acceptance criteria were self-set and self-graded.
- **Repairs for Whom?** — pre-registered confirmation → the plan was pre-specified but never independently timestamped or publicly registered, and the methods it beats appear only as re-implemented verification steps, not full pipelines.
- **Security Impact of Coding Agent Memory Files** — memory files steer agents toward vulnerable code → secure-completion shifts measured −8 to +6 percentage points, no consistent pattern, indistinguishable from chance at this scale (runs not repeated). The entry point is demonstrated; reliable steering is not.

### What the briefing could not yet see

- The day's verifiers and benchmarks grade on home ground — same-group benchmarks, a self-cleaned dataset, self-reviewed tasks — [[concepts/evaluator-circularity]] and [[concepts/ground-truth-fallibility]].
- "Independent" and "verified" turned out to be scoped words: VeriCUA's independence stops at the executing agent; Auctopus's criteria are its own — [[concepts/scoped-assurance]].
- Reported margins partly measure how the comparator was built, as ExploreRepair's weakened baselines show — [[concepts/comparator-construction-shapes-margins]].
- The signal's second half met its counterweight: ungated accumulated memory moved secure completions by −8 to +6 points, chance-like. The durable pattern is gated, inspectable state — [[concepts/improvement-lives-in-external-state]].
