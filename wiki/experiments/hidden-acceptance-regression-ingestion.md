# Hidden-acceptance and regression-preserving ingestion evaluation

**Status: proposed; not executed.** Derived from FINCOSMOS, EvoAudit, EnvOpt and the AI-written software assurance case. The studies did not jointly validate this protocol.

## Hypothesis

A versioned strategy library plus independently executed source-validation gates will improve fully accepted knowledge entries without losing previously correct relationships. An optimized wiki environment may reduce tokens, but must pass the same acceptance gate.

## Inputs and controls

Prepare 30 source-backed automotive cybersecurity ingestion tasks with one to three hidden requirements each, plus 20 untouched transfer tasks. Freeze source snapshots and independent answer keys. Compare (A) current single-agent ingestion, (B) strategy-library agent, (C) strategy-library plus optimized information environment. Match model, budget and tool permissions. Repeat with random task order.

## Fault injection

Plant missing publication dates, duplicate canonical aliases, false numerical claims, omitted citations, silently skipped validators, unknown write outcomes and regression-inducing relationship edits. Ensure the evaluator is isolated from candidate outputs until acceptance.

## Metrics and acceptance

Report independent full-task acceptance rate, relation precision and recall, new cases solved, previously correct cases lost, source traceability, actual validator execution coverage, abstention rate, token spend and wall-clock time. A candidate cannot be promoted if it introduces a critical source-integrity regression or fails to execute a required validator. Record all excluded tasks and failed checks; do not turn a missing result into a pass.
