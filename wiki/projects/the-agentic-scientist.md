# The Agentic Scientist

**Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18933. **Date:** October 13, 2026 (older research).

## Mechanism

The authors describe staged research from idea generation through sandboxed experiments, baseline and ablation construction, analysis and reporting. A meta-controller coordinates workers and checks stage rubrics. See [[techniques/embodied-and-scientific-agents]] for the related mechanism and its evaluation boundaries.

## Evidence limits

The abstract reports one accepted collaborative ALIFE paper; this is not a general measure of scientific validity. Full methodology and independent replication were not verified.

## Proposed experiment

Compare single-agent and orchestrated runs on five known-answer experiments, with fixed budget and independent scoring of conclusions and artifacts. Not executed.
