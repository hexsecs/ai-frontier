# AutoSR: Automatic Symbolic Regression by Searching Research States

**Original source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16876  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Evidence status:** Author-reported research; not independently replicated in this knowledge base.

## Mechanism

An automated scientific-discovery system that searches persistent research states containing candidate equations, motivations, code, diagnostics, failed trials and independent reviewer findings. It uses progressive-widening Monte Carlo tree search.

Branch-local research memory plus asynchronous proposer/reviewer experiments and evidence-preserving tree search.

## Significance and comparison

Searches auditable investigations, not just candidate outputs. Authors report exact recovery on nine selected benchmark challenges, including three unsolved by twelve compared systems.

A scientific analogue of provenance-aware coding agents: experimental criticism survives into future search decisions.

## Practical experiment

Apply research-state trees to three automotive-security hypotheses; require each branch to preserve failed tests, source excerpts, counterevidence and reviewer notes.

## Limits

Older exploratory selection; nine selected cases are feasibility evidence, not a demonstrated broad generalization or repeated-run reliability.
