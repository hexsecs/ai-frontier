# TIGA

The November 10 preprint describes explicit taxonomy state, constrained edit tools, interaction memory and adaptive policy. Across four taxonomies and two model backbones, authors report generally better structural quality, mixed precision and higher cost. Global review benefits vary. Abstract-level evidence; no independent replication cited.

Compare constrained editing with unrestricted edits on structural validity, semantic accuracy and cost. Structural validity is narrower than [[concepts/verified-completion-vs-apparent-success|independent acceptance]].
