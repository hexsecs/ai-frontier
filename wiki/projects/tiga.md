# TIGA

The November 10 preprint describes explicit taxonomy state, constrained edit tools, interaction memory and adaptive policy. The authors evaluate four benchmark datasets. In Section 6.3 and Table 3, removing interaction memory lowers edge F1 on both WordNet and TExEval-2. Adding global review improves that metric on WordNet and slightly reduces it on TExEval-2 under the reported revision cap. Section 6.4 reports higher inference costs than the baselines. These are author-reported results. No independent replication is cited.

Compare constrained editing with unrestricted edits on structural validity, semantic accuracy and cost. Structural validity is narrower than [[concepts/verified-completion-vs-apparent-success|independent acceptance]].
