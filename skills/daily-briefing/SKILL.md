# daily-briefing

Read AGENTS.md, kb.yaml, automation/briefing-policy.yaml, docs/FIGURES.md, index.md, seven latest available briefs and relevant indexes. Use Detroit date. Inspect open ingestion PRs and missing dates; invoke recovery first. Discover 5–10 substantive primary-source findings, 70–80% core engineering/research and 20–30% exploratory; label older discoveries and update existing records for material changes. Invoke source-verification for each, arxiv-reading for papers, knowledge-ingestion for accepted findings and publication for the resulting change. Preserve complete narrative including mechanism, novelty, evidence limits, practical implications and bounded experiments. On quiet days publish fewer verified findings rather than padding. Do not stop at research output. Report actual archive, canonical entities, synthesis, PR, checks, merge and deployment separately. If blocked, preserve verified work and resume oldest missing stage next run.
